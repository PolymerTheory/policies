Playlist Toolkit for YouTube

Privacy Policy · Effective 24 October 2026

This independent Chrome extension helps you select, filter, copy, reorder and remove entries in your YouTube playlists, including Watch Later, manage watched status for selected videos, and select videos from your YouTube Subscriptions feed to add to playlists. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Information used to provide the features

On YouTube playlist and Subscriptions pages, the extension processes playlist and video identifiers, titles, channel names, video durations, watch-progress information, playlist order, and playlist URLs you enter. It uses the YouTube account/session configuration available in the current tab. To authenticate requests to YouTube, it reads a YouTube session cookie and computes an authorization hash locally. The browser also sends the normal YouTube session cookies with these same-origin requests. Cookies and credentials are not saved by the extension or sent to the developer.

This information is used only to display selection controls, find matching or duplicate entries, read playlists and perform playlist and watched-status changes you request. The extension does not collect a browser-wide history or monitor other websites. Playlist-page selections are kept temporarily in page memory and cleared when the page is refreshed or left. On Subscriptions, selected video identifiers are also saved in this tab’s YouTube sessionStorage, with a hash of the non-secret account identity and a timestamp, to restore selections after Back or reload. They are not sent to Chrome Sync or the developer. Restoration expires after 24 hours; expired records are ignored and pruned on subsequent saves. Clear selection removes the current account’s remembered selections, including videos not currently loaded. These records are accessible to scripts on the YouTube page; no cookies or authentication credentials are stored in them. Recent changes keep up to five before/after playlist snapshots in page memory for undo and are cleared when the page is refreshed or left. You can explicitly download a playlist snapshot or a change backup as a JSON file; those files may include video titles, identifiers and order. They are saved only where you download them and are not sent to the developer.

Watched status and YouTube watch history

When you choose Mark watched, the extension requests playback metadata for the selected videos and sends playback/watch-time reports directly to YouTube using your signed-in session. It does not open a player window. YouTube may round or delay recorded progress; the extension verifies a reported value above 50% before claiming success. This changes watch history and progress across your YouTube account and may affect recommendations. Videos already reported above 50% are skipped.

When you confirm Mark unwatched, the extension reads and searches your YouTube watch history to identify the selected videos, sends their individual history-removal actions, and checks history absence and cleared progress. This removes prior views of those videos too; it is not an exact undo of their former progress. Other history entries returned during these checks are processed only in page memory to identify the selected videos, not saved or sent to the developer. Check watched status only reads fresh playlist progress and does not send playback reports or remove history.

Saved settings and Chrome Sync

The extension saves your filter settings (including entered text), watched-percentage shortcut, channel-group names, channel lists, default-open behavior, subscriptions checkbox/card selection mode, default destination playlist identifier, toolbar pins and number appearance in Chrome extension local storage. When Chrome Sync is enabled for your Chrome profile, these preferences are also synchronized through Google's Chrome Sync service. Google processes that synchronization under its own policies. A local pending-sync flag helps preserve settings if synchronization fails. Full playlists, cookies, credentials and diagnostics are not saved in these preferences.

Where information goes

Playlist, playback-report and history requests go directly from your browser to YouTube to provide your requested playlist and watched-status operations. Preferences may pass through Chrome Sync as described above. The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising or developer telemetry. The playback reports described above go only to YouTube for your requested watched-status changes. The developer does not receive or sell your data, and it is not used for advertising, credit decisions or unrelated purposes.

Your choices and retention

You control selection, playlist edits and watched-status changes. Removal requires confirmation. You can edit or remove saved channel groups and change filters in the extension. To replace all saved preferences with defaults, use Backup / transfer settings and import the following JSON:

{"version":1,"watchedPercent":50,"lastFilter":null,"channelGroups":[]}

Disabling Chrome Sync stops synchronization under Chrome's controls. Uninstalling removes local extension storage; Chrome/Google controls retention of synced data, so uninstalling should not be treated as a guaranteed cloud-data deletion or backup method. You can export settings to a JSON file; exported files remain wherever you save them until you delete them. Do not put secrets in filters or group names: the in-page UI and its settings bridge are accessible to scripts running on the YouTube page.

Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is used only for the playlist-management and watched-status features described here.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when privacy-related behavior changes. Questions or requests can be submitted through the publisher's public policy issue tracker. Do not include credentials or private playlist contents in public issues.
