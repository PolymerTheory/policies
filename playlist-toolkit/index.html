Playlist Toolkit for YouTube

Privacy Policy · Effective 17 October 2026

This independent Chrome extension helps you select, filter, copy, reorder and remove entries in your YouTube playlists, including Watch Later. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Information used to provide the features

On youtube.com, the extension processes playlist and video identifiers, titles, channel names, video durations, watch-progress information, playlist order, and playlist URLs you enter. It uses the YouTube account/session configuration available in the current tab. To authenticate requests to YouTube, it reads a YouTube session cookie and computes an authorization hash locally. The browser also sends the normal YouTube session cookies with these same-origin requests. Cookies and credentials are not saved by the extension or sent to the developer.

This information is used only to display selection controls, find matching or duplicate entries, read playlists and perform playlist changes you request. The extension does not collect a browser-wide history or monitor other websites. Selection and playlist data are kept temporarily in page memory; selection is cleared when the page is refreshed or left. Recent changes keep up to five before/after playlist snapshots in page memory for undo and are cleared when the page is refreshed or left. You can explicitly download a playlist snapshot or a change backup as a JSON file; those files may include video titles, identifiers and order. They are saved only where you download them and are not sent to the developer.

Saved settings and Chrome Sync

The extension saves your filter settings (including entered text), watched-percentage shortcut, channel-group names, channel lists, default-open behavior, toolbar pins and number appearance in Chrome extension local storage. When Chrome Sync is enabled for your Chrome profile, these preferences are also synchronized through Google's Chrome Sync service. Google processes that synchronization under its own policies. A local pending-sync flag helps preserve settings if synchronization fails. Full playlists, cookies, credentials and diagnostics are not saved in these preferences.

Where information goes

Playlist requests go directly from your browser to YouTube to read playlists and perform your requested edits. Preferences may pass through Chrome Sync as described above. The extension has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or telemetry. The developer does not receive or sell your data, and it is not used for advertising, credit decisions or unrelated purposes.

Your choices and retention

You control selection and playlist edits. Removal requires confirmation. You can edit or remove saved channel groups and change filters in the extension. To replace all saved preferences with defaults, use Backup / transfer settings and import the following JSON:

{"version":1,"watchedPercent":50,"lastFilter":null,"channelGroups":[]}

Disabling Chrome Sync stops synchronization under Chrome's controls. Uninstalling removes local extension storage; Chrome/Google controls retention of synced data, so uninstalling should not be treated as a guaranteed cloud-data deletion or backup method. You can export settings to a JSON file; exported files remain wherever you save them until you delete them. Do not put secrets in filters or group names: the in-page UI and its settings bridge are accessible to scripts running on the YouTube page.

Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. User data is used only for the playlist-management features described here.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when privacy-related behavior changes. Questions or requests can be submitted through the publisher's public policy issue tracker. Do not include credentials or private playlist contents in public issues.
